What it does

Most trip planners want a finished itinerary on day one. A family doesn't have one. It has school holidays, a points balance, three hotel tabs open and a confirmation email somewhere. MaiCation starts there.

Ideas first, bookings later

A flight, a hotel, a note and a task are all the same kind of thing. They start as ideas, get shortlisted, held, then booked. Booking one option puts the others away.

It reads your booking emails

Connect a mailbox and confirmations are found and offered for review, including the ones your partner forwarded. Nothing goes on the itinerary until you say so.

An assistant that suggests, never books

Ask it about the trip, hand it a booking PDF or a fare screenshot. It proposes a change and you apply or reject it. It can't spend your money.

A memo the evening before

At 6pm, tomorrow's bookings with their references, where tonight's bed is, the forecast, and when to leave for the first thing.

Every price says when it was seen

A fare or a room rate is a snapshot with a date on it. Nothing is shown as live availability, because "A$2,950 seen 20 July" and "A$2,950" are different facts.

A vault for the passports

Passports, visas, insurance and emergency contacts, encrypted, behind a second passphrase. It locks itself after ten minutes.

Share the plan, not the numbers

A share link or an exported page has no booking references, loyalty numbers or passport details. The grandparents get the itinerary and nothing else.

Works without a signal

Pages you've opened are kept on the phone and shown when roaming is off, with a note saying so. The budget keeps working too.

On the phone, once you're away

The day page

One screen per day. What's booked, in local time, with the reference the desk will ask for. Underneath, the memo that goes out the evening before: where you'll be, what's checking in, what still needs paying.

Passport and document numbers never appear in the memo. They stay in the vault behind its own passphrase.

Add the site to the phone's home screen and it behaves like an app. The same account works on the iPad on the couch and the laptop at 10pm.

Questions

Does it book anything for me?

No. The assistant can set up a search with your dates and party and hand you the live site, but it can't make a booking or spend money. Every price it shows is a snapshot with its date, not a hold.

What does the email reader actually read?

It lists sender, subject and date, reads a body only when the message already looks like travel, and keeps only travel emails. Everything else leaves an anonymous fingerprint so it isn't looked at twice. Every decision is listed and you can override any of them.

Can grandparents see the plan?

Yes, through a share link or an exported page. Both are built from a view with no booking references, loyalty numbers, full names or contact details. People on the trip can be invited to edit it.

What happens when the pass ends?

Everything stays and stays editable. The email reader keeps working and the assistant goes back to the free 30 questions. Nothing is deleted and nothing renews.

Can I use my own Claude or ChatGPT instead?

Yes. In ChatGPT, add the MaiCation GPT and sign in from the chat. In Claude, add the connector from Set up. Your own assistant can read the trip, search stays and flights and propose changes for you to approve. Nothing runs on our side, so there's no charge from us.
