Why it works

Your professional hour is worth more than you think

Spending an hour volunteering in person is a generous act. But for most professionals, donating that hour's earnings to an effective charity creates far more measurable impact. This is the idea behind 'earning to give' — researched extensively by 80,000 Hours and the effective altruism community. GiveWell's cost-effectiveness analyses show how far a single dollar goes at the world's top charities. ProBono makes it easy to put this into practice.

In practice — an illustration

Jenny starts her week by pledging Monday's first hour to an eye-health charity. By the end of her morning meeting, that hour's pay could cover sight-restoring cataract surgery for up to three people — without taking a single day off work.

1 hr  ·  up to 3 surgeries  ·  0 days off

Jenny is illustrative; the arithmetic isn't. The Fred Hollows Foundation reports cataract surgery can cost as little as $25 in some countries. ProBono isn't affiliated with any charity — you give to them directly.

How it works

Five steps, and the money never touches us

01
Make a pledge
Decide how often you want to dedicate a working hour to charity — weekly, fortnightly, or monthly.
02
Choose your charity
Select from recommended high-impact charities, or add your own favourite cause.
03
Set your hourly rate
Use the built-in calculator or estimate your own hourly wage to work out what you'll set aside each cycle.
04
Work like normal
When your hour arrives you'll be notified. Work even better knowing this hour is for charity.
05
Give — directly by design
Make the donation you pledged, straight to the charity. ProBono reminds you and keeps your record — it never handles the money. Your giving stays between you and the cause.
