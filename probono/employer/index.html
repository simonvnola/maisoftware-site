Give an hour through your workplace

ProBono is a free iPhone app. Pledge one working hour to a charity and it writes the workplace giving request to payroll for you.

  1. Get ProBono from the App Store.
  2. Come back to this page and tap "Open in ProBono".
  3. Your employer's details are filled in. Choose a charity and pledge your hour.
