What MaiCollection does

Every collection gets
its own shape

A record collector tracks pressings and sleeve condition. A card collector tracks set, grade and serial number. MaiCollection builds each collection around the details that matter for that kind of thing.

Set up from a sentence

Type what you collect. AI proposes the fields worth tracking, how to group the collection and how to show it. You change anything you like before it is created, or start from a template or a blank page.

Add from a photo

Photograph a shelf or a single piece. AI proposes the items it can see and fills in what it can; extra photos of a back, base or label add detail. You review every row before it is saved.

Add from a receipt

Hand over an invoice as a photo or a PDF and each line becomes a proposed item, with what you paid and where you bought it. Only the lines you confirm are saved; the receipt itself is not kept.

Identify with the camera

Out at a market or a shop? Tap the camera, take the picture, and AI proposes what the item is. Then choose: add it to the collection, or put it on the wishlist.

A layout for each collection

List, grid, gallery or table, chosen per collection. Sort and group by any of its fields, so cards can sit by set and records by decade.

Value estimates

Ask for an estimate on an item and AI searches the web, then gives a figure with a line on how it got there. Every AI figure is marked est. and stays editable. It is a guide, not a valuation for insurance or sale.

A wishlist beside every collection

Keep what you are hunting for next to what you own, with a priority and a note. When you add something that looks like a wishlist item, the app asks before taking it off the list.

Share the wishlist with family

Send a link that opens in any browser, no app or account needed. It shows what you are hoping for and your notes, and never prices, values or what you already own. Turn the link off whenever you like.

Find deals when you ask

Tap “Find deals” and AI searches for current offers on your wishlist items. Each one links to the retailer and shows the date it was found. Prices change, so check the retailer’s page before you buy.

Suggestions for what is missing

Ask for ideas and AI suggests items your wishlist does not have yet. A suggestion becomes a wishlist item only when you tap to add it.

Collection ideas

Start from layouts other collectors have shared for their kind of collection. Sharing your own is a tick box that starts unticked, and only the layout goes: never your items, photos, notes or values.

Duplicate check

Adding something you may already own? The app flags a matching name before it goes in. It only ever asks or flags; nothing is removed or skipped without your say.
One account, everything you own

Works with Maicasa

MaiCollection uses the same account as Maicasa, our home manager. If you choose to, the value of your collections can appear in Maicasa’s contents inventory as a single linked row.

Your total, in your home inventory

Switch on the Maicasa link in Settings and Maicasa shows one row: how many items and their total estimated value. The link is off until you turn it on, each collection can be left out, and the items themselves stay in MaiCollection.

One sign-in, one allowance

Sign in with the account you use for Maicasa, or create one in the app. AI requests in MaiCollection count toward the same weekly allowance as Maicasa, and nothing calls AI unless you ask it to.
Who it’s for

For the collector,
and the people who buy for them

Whether it is three shelves or three hundred boxes, the app keeps the record. The wishlist makes birthdays easier for everyone else.

Collectors
Know what you have, what condition it is in, what you paid and what it may be worth now, without building a spreadsheet for every kind of thing you collect.
Family and friends
Open the shared wishlist and see exactly which piece is wanted, in which size or edition, and what matters most. No more guessing, and no doubles.

AI proposes.
You decide.
