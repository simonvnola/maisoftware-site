Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated November 2026 · Applies to the MaiCollection app

The short version
  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your collections and the photos you add
  • No advertising, no tracking and no analytics software in the app
  • Photos and details go to AI only when you ask for an AI feature
  • Nothing is shared with other people unless you turn sharing on
  • We never sell your data
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time

1. Who we are

MaiCollection is a product of Mai Software, operated as a sole-trader business registered in Australia under an ABN. References to "Mai Software," "we," "us," or "our" refer to that business.

Contact: info@maisoftware.app

2. One account with Maicasa

MaiCollection uses the same account as Maicasa, our home manager app. You sign in with your Maicasa account or create one in MaiCollection, and it works in both apps. This policy covers what MaiCollection does; the Maicasa Privacy Policy covers what you do in Maicasa.

Because it is one account, deleting it removes it from both apps (see Section 10).

3. What we collect

  • Account information — your email address, an account ID and sign-in tokens. You can sign in with an email address and password (the password is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider) or with Sign in with Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the email address Apple passes on, which may be a private relay address
  • Your collections — the collections you create and their descriptions, and the items in them: names, details, condition, notes, quantities, purchase prices and dates, where you bought them, and value estimates. An item added from a receipt also keeps the retailer and invoice number, so the same receipt is not added twice
  • Your wishlist — the items you are hoping for, their priority, your notes and any target price, plus deals found for them (retailer, link, price and the date found)
  • Photos — the photos of items you choose to add, stored privately in your account
  • Settings — your currency, and whether you have turned on the Maicasa link or a shared wishlist link
  • AI usage counts — we keep a weekly count of the AI requests on your account, with their size and which feature and AI model they were for. We use this to apply the weekly allowance. We do not keep a copy of what was sent or what came back, other than the results you choose to save
  • Technical logs — our hosting providers keep standard server logs, such as IP address and the time of a request, to run and secure the service

Receipts and invoices you hand to the app are read to propose items and are not saved to your account. Only the items you confirm are kept.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, payment card details or an advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics software. Wishlist suggestions are kept on your device only.

4. How we use it

  • To provide the app: storing your collections and showing them on your devices
  • To run the AI features you ask for (Section 5)
  • To apply the weekly allowance on AI requests, which is shared with Maicasa
  • To show a wishlist to the people you send its link to (Section 6)
  • To show your collections' total in Maicasa, if you turn that on (Section 8)
  • To respond to support and privacy requests

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

5. AI processing — Anthropic Claude

MaiCollection uses the Claude API by Anthropic PBC, a company based in the United States. AI runs only when you ask for it. Nothing is sent in the background. What is sent depends on the feature:

  • Setting up a collection — the description you type
  • Adding or identifying from a photo — the photos you choose, and the collection's name and fields so the answer fits
  • Adding from a receipt — the photo or PDF of the receipt or invoice, and the collection's name and fields. A receipt can show personal details such as a name or address; the AI is instructed not to return card numbers, account numbers, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses, and the app removes anything shaped like a card number from what comes back
  • Value estimates — the item's name, details and condition, and the collection's name and fields
  • Deals — the names and details of the wishlist items you are searching for, any target price you have set, and the collection's name and fields
  • Wishlist suggestions — the names and details of the items you own and the items on your wishlist in that collection, so the AI can see what is missing, plus the names of suggestions you have already had
  • For value estimates, deals and suggestions, Anthropic also runs web searches based on those details

Your email address and account ID are not included in what is sent to Anthropic.

Anthropic's data handling is governed by their Privacy Policy. We use the API under terms that restrict Anthropic from using your inputs to train their models.

AI can be wrong. What it proposes is shown to you to check before it is saved to your collection, and value estimates are marked as estimates. Deals are the exception: offers found for your wishlist are saved as found, with the date, and you can dismiss them.

6. Shared wishlist links

You can create a link to a collection's wishlist and send it to family or friends. Anyone who has the link can open the page, without an account, so share it only with people you want to see it.

  • The page shows the collection's name and its wishlist items: name, details, priority, your wishlist note and the first photo
  • It never shows purchase prices, target prices, value estimates, any field you have set up as a money amount, or anything you already own
  • We record when a link was created and when it was last opened, and show you this in the app. We do not record who opened it, apart from the standard server logs our hosting providers keep (Section 3)
  • You can turn a link off at any time in the app, and the page stops working straight away. Deleting the collection turns its link off too. Photo addresses from a page someone already had open can keep working for up to a day

7. Collection ideas (shared layouts)

When AI sets up a collection for you, you can choose to share its layout with other collectors by ticking a box. The box is unticked every time, and nothing is shared unless you tick it.

  • What is shared is the layout only: a general title (such as "Fountain pens"), an icon, and the field names with their choices and units
  • Never shared: your collection's name or description, your items, photos, notes or values, or your name or email address
  • Other collectors cannot see who a layout came from. We keep a private link between a layout and the account that offered it, so that limits can apply; that link is removed if you delete your account
  • If you report a layout, we keep a record that your account reported it, so that limits can apply. It is deleted with your account
  • Layouts are checked before anyone else sees them. This check is automated and uses AI: only the layout text is sent, never your account details
  • A shared layout stays available after you delete your collection or your account, because it holds no personal information. The app has no button to withdraw one; to have a layout you shared removed, email us and we will remove it

8. Sharing with Maicasa

In Settings you can turn on a link to Maicasa. It is off unless you turn it on. While it is on, your Maicasa contents inventory shows one summary row for your collections: the number of items, their total estimated value, your currency and the date it was calculated. You can leave individual collections out of the total.

The items themselves are not copied into Maicasa. Turning the link off removes the summary row.

9. Storage, security and who we share data with

Your account, collections and photos are stored with Supabase, Inc., on servers in Sydney, Australia. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules in the database limit each account to its own collections, and photos are held in private storage.

We share data only with the providers that run the service:

  • Supabase, Inc. — database, photo storage and sign-in, in Australia
  • Anthropic PBC — to process AI requests, in the United States (Section 5)
  • Apple — if you use Sign in with Apple, and for distributing the app through the App Store. If sign-in with Google is offered and you choose it, Google confirms your identity to us in the same way
  • Vercel Inc. — hosts this website and delivers shared wishlist pages; requests pass through its servers, which may be outside Australia, including in the United States. Our website's marketing pages count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify visitors. The app, this page and shared wishlist pages do not use it
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where required by applicable law

This means some information is handled outside Australia, mainly in the United States when you use an AI feature, and possibly when someone opens a shared wishlist page.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at info@maisoftware.app.

10. Deleting your data

  • An item, a photo or a collection — delete it in the app and it disappears from the app straight away. Deleted items and collections, and the photos attached to them, are kept out of view until you delete your account or ask us to erase them. A photo you remove from an item you keep is deleted from storage
  • Your account — in MaiCollection, go to Settings and choose Delete account. This is the same account as Maicasa, so it deletes your Maicasa account too, with everything in both apps. The app tells you this and asks you to confirm. It cannot be undone
  • What happens then — your account and the records linked to it (collections, items, wishlist, deals, settings and AI usage counts) are deleted straight away. Photo files are removed from storage within 30 days. Wishlist suggestions kept on your device are removed with the account

If you cannot use the app, email info@maisoftware.app from your account's email address and we will delete the account for you.

11. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Complain about a breach to the relevant privacy regulator in your country

To exercise any of these rights, email info@maisoftware.app. We will respond within 30 days.

12. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, or ask us to delete your data, we remove it as described in Section 10 and in any case within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something. Server logs kept by our hosting providers expire on their own schedules. Shared layouts (Section 7) hold no personal information and may be kept.

13. Children's privacy

MaiCollection is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Contact us if you believe we have, and we will delete it promptly.

14. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be notified in the app or by email. The version number and date at the top always show the current version.
