Software for the things you keep.

Mai Software makes three iPhone apps, and each one is a careful record-keeper: the house and its upkeep, the cellar and the bar beside it, and the giving you've promised yourself.

The apps — three entries 01 Maicasa the home 02 MaiCellar cellar & bar 03 ProBono giving

Entry 01 · Maicasa

The home, finally under control.

Maicasa is a home manager for owners and landlords. It works out which jobs are worth doing first, builds the list from a scan of your property, plans the budget, and keeps track of what's inside.

Quotes, tradies, due dates and costs live in one record instead of a drawer of paper.

Entry 02 · MaiCellar

The cellar book — and the bar.

One app with two sides. The cellar side scans labels to catalogue your wine and keeps each bottle's story: vintage, value, and when it will drink best.

The bar side — MaiBar — looks at the spirits you actually own and shows the cocktails you can make tonight, with every measure scaled to the number of drinks.

Entry 03 · ProBono

Give one hour of what you earn.

ProBono asks a single thing: pledge the value of one working hour — each week, fortnight or month — to a cause you choose. It reminds you when the day arrives and keeps your record straight.

The money never passes through the app. You give directly to the charity, and that's by design.
