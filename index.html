Entry 01 · Maicasa

The home, finally under control.

Maicasa is a home manager for owners and landlords. It works out which jobs are worth doing first, builds the list from a scan of your property, plans the budget, and keeps track of what's inside.

Quotes, tradies, due dates and costs live in one record instead of a drawer of paper.

Entry 02 · MaiCellar

The cellar book — and the bar.

One app with two sides. The cellar side scans labels to catalogue your wine and keeps each bottle's story: vintage, value, and when it will drink best.

The bar side — MaiBar — looks at the spirits you actually own and shows the cocktails you can make tonight, with every measure scaled to the number of drinks.

Entry 03 · ProBono

Give one hour of what you earn.

ProBono asks a single thing: pledge the value of one working hour — each week, fortnight or month — to a cause you choose. It reminds you when the day arrives and keeps your record straight.

The money never passes through the app. You give directly to the charity, and that's by design.

Entry 04 · MaiCaddy

The golf bag, looked after.

MaiCaddy tracks the wear on your grips, gloves and wedge grooves from how much you actually play — rounds and range buckets both count — and speaks up only when a club genuinely needs a hand.

It keeps each club's record and value too, so the whole bag doubles as a ready-made insurance list.

Entry 05 · MaiCation · on the web

The family trip, from loose ideas to the day itself.

MaiCation lets a trip stay a pile of ideas and reward-seat sightings for as long as it needs to, then hardens it into bookings. An assistant that proposes and never acts alone, an email reader that finds the bookings you already made, an encrypted vault for the passports, and a memo each evening for the day ahead.

Every price says when it was seen. Runs in the browser on the phone, the iPad and the laptop, and everyone in the family signs in to the same trip. Fourteen days free.

Mai Software is an independent studio in Australia. We build small, careful apps — for iPhone, and now for the browser — and maintain them ourselves: the same people who write the code answer the support mail.

Also from the studio · on the web

RegattaHQ — race day, run properly.

Also for the browser rather than the iPhone. Regatta HQ is a modern regatta manager for rowing clubs and associations: entries, the programme, the lane draw, results and live results, all in one place.

Built on the web so it runs on the phone at the finish line and the laptop in the tower — nothing to install. The app will be hosted right here when it's ready.
