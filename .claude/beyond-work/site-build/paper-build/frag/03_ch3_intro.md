## 3. A picture of 2045

This chapter describes what a basic income could make possible by 2045. It is a picture of the pathway, not a forecast: it happens only if the pathway is triggered and every test is passed. AI must displace enough work to meet the need test, the Australian trial must find a fall in participation under 3.0 points, the PBO must certify the funding, and Parliament must then pass a new Act setting a rate and keep it. A full pension-rate basic income also needs AI-driven growth far above the Budget's baseline, and on the paper's figures it cannot be funded from income tax (Section 6). On the central path none of this happens, and Australia in 2045 has the recommended Stage 1: the floor at the poverty line, the child payment and the higher Rent Assistance. The pathway is insurance, and like most insurance it may never be used.

The seven people below are fictional and live on one path, chosen to show the pathway working. A severe AI shock from 2032-33 meets the need test early in 2035, and the floor holds its value until then. The trial has found a fall in participation under 3 points, the PBO certifies, and Parliament legislates a payment at the JobSeeker rate from July 2035. On the 22 October paper's run that needed a rise of every income-tax rate of 25.5 points with a dividend fund to lend, or 26.9 points without one, and there is no Stage 3 by 2045-46 (Tables 6.38 and 6.52). Duc and Tom are single, so the floor reaches them; a displaced worker whose partner earns above its cut-out would get nothing until the universal payment starts.
