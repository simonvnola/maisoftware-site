The system ties security to paid work through means tests and mutual obligation. Payments are withdrawn as income rises, a partner's earnings can remove a payment altogether, and the activity test suspends payments: in January to March 2024, 38% of people in Workforce Australia had a payment suspended and 4.3% had one cancelled [S60]. Means tests create errors and debts, and at their worst Robodebt raised about A$1.76bn in alleged debts against about 526,000 people [S56]. Much of the work that holds families and communities together is unpaid: carers provide about 2.2 billion hours of care a year, worth A$77.9bn [S55]. The paper's targets for this part of the problem are in Table 2.2: poverty after housing costs of 12.0% or lower for all persons, 7.0% or lower for children and 55.5% or lower in JobSeeker-reliant households by 2030-31.
