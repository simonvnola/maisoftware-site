### The risk to the funding: a joint stress test

The central levy date rests on every assumption holding at once, and they will not. A joint stress test draws them together, 4,000 times (Table 6.9). Each is drawn from a triangular distribution over the paper's own tested range: the five taxes' yield (80% to 120% of their costings), the floor's caseload response (elasticity 0.21 to 0.56), the response to the child payment's taper (0.1 to 0.4) and the labour response (0.05 to 0.29). Cost overruns of nil to A$2bn a year, the floor costed on the model's or the PBO's basis, and the household model's three calibrations are drawn too. AI displacement follows the paper's moderate or severe scenario in 10% and 5% of draws, an assumption, because the paper has no probabilities for them.

On P\* the chance of a levy before 2044-45 is 48% to 74%, and before 2034-35 37% to 59%. The low ends draw only the symmetric risks (yield, behaviour and calibration); the high ends add cost overruns, the PBO's floor costing and AI displacement. The first levy is most often in 2032-33, because borrowing not repaid by 30 June 2032 is levied at once; in 22% of draws there is no levy to 2045-46. P\* carries the same funding risk as Part 1 (47% to 73% before 2044-45); the targeted package carries more (70% to 87%). The 59% and 45% in the revision plan came from a first run that could not be reproduced; these figures replace them.

So the levy is better read as a range than as a date. The central path is the case in which every assumption lands on its central value at once; the range in Table 6.9 is the better guide.

### Sensitivity: one assumption at a time

Stage 1 has no fund to absorb a shortfall, so its levy moves with every assumption. On Part 1's run the five taxes had to yield 99.7% of their costings for no levy before 2044-45. At 90% the levy started in 2032-33 and peaked at 1.82% of market income; at 80% it totalled A$370.8bn to 2045-46 and peaked at 4.41%, or started in 2029-30 and peaked at 1.49% if the borrowing is repaid over five years.
