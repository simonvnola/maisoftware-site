# Funding options for P* beyond the five taxes (10 November 2026)

Generated by scripts/affordability_options.py from runs/fiscal.json and affordability_inputs.json. Every figure is first-round and provisional. The script reproduces P*'s saved funding path (first levy 2043-44, levies of A$36.5bn, borrowing of A$20.7bn, break-even yield 100.2%) and every year of the phased start before it adds anything.

What this does not do: it gives no poverty rate, no count of who gains or loses and no effective tax rate, because the household model is not in this repository. The new revenue lines are not PBO costings. The low, central and high cases move the author's assumptions (behaviour haircut, recovered share, rate) together.

Options A and B have no added revenue, so they have no range. The balance is the surplus the rule accumulates at the debt rate if nothing else is done with it; it is not a target, and it shows how much the added measures exceed what P* needs. The top-up's central cost, A$6.1bn in 2028-29, is the midpoint of Table 6.58's range, and in G it starts in 2029-30 with the child payment. In F and G the low case pairs low revenue with the top-up's high cost, and the high case the reverse. The paper's own break-even, 100.2%, is for no levy before 2044-45; on the stricter test here (none to 2045-46) option A needs 101.7%.

The fund holds A$551.7bn at 30 June 2046 (A$336.7bn in 2026 dollars) and pays Stage 1 A$20.7bn in 2045-46, against the A$20.9bn the two measures raise that year. With the four added measures, Stage 1 borrows A$8.3bn in 2028-29 and needs no levy to 2045-46 in the central and high cases; in the low case it levies A$12.0bn from 2032-33. Without them Stage 1 needs a levy: the first levy is 2032-33 and levies total A$323.5bn. The return is the paper's central assumption, 6.5% nominal [S132]; the borrowing in the early years costs the debt rate, 4.13%, so the fund gains only if it earns more than that.

## The universal pathway

In 2032-33 the four measures raise A$38.9bn (A$21.0bn to A$64.0bn). At Table 6.40's static A$20.75bn a point, that is 1.9 points (1.0 to 3.1) of the 12.0 points Stage 2 needs on every income-tax rate, if none of it were used for Stage 1.
