What it does

One record for the whole regatta.

Built to replace legacy regatta software, keep-for-keep, and then move past it. A single source of truth from the association down to the individual lane — entries and crews, the event programme, the lane draw, times, places and margins, and a live view for everyone on the bank.

01 · Entries & crews

Take entries, manage crews

Clubs enter events, crews are built and seated, and late entries, substitutions and scratchings are handled without a paper scramble.

02 · Programme & draw

Build the day, draw the lanes

Compose the event programme, then run a lane draw. Heats, reps and progressions are built into the schema, ready as the day grows.

03 · Results & live

Times, places, official

Enter times, compute places and margins, and make a race official — while a realtime live view updates for spectators as it happens.

Under the hood

Built to be run all season.

A Next.js web app with a Supabase back end — a full domain model from associations and clubs down to races and lanes, with realtime results and role-based access. Reserved boundaries are already in place for a rules-as-data pointscore engine and for timing hardware and photo-finish, so they can plug in without a rewrite. Historical legacy-system IDs are preserved end-to-end, so past data and links survive the move.

Realtime

Live results on the bank

Results and races publish over realtime subscriptions — the seed of a spectator view that anyone can follow from their phone.

Migration

Keeps your history

Legacy system references are kept on athletes and regattas, so a season of records imports cleanly rather than starting from zero.

Extensible

Room to grow

Heats and progressions, pointscore and timing integration are designed as additive follow-ups — the app is complete without them and better with them.
