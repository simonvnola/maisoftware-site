01 — THE PRO SHOP

A pro shop
in your pocket.

When something in your bag does need a hand, the Pro Shop tab knows what kind — and points you to the right care, not a storefront.

CLN

Club cleaning

Products and how-to guides for grooves, grips and faces.

FIT

Club fitting

Find a fitter near you when a club has never quite felt right.

RPR

Club repair

Shafts, hosels, grooves and loft/lie — sorted before they cost you shots.

02 — WHAT IT DOES

Five quiet jobs, running in the background of your golf.

01

Reads your whole bag

Clubs, glove, shoes and rangefinder in one top-down view — each with its own record, value and a live status dot.

02

Estimates the wear for you

Grips soften, gloves stretch, wedge grooves lose their bite. Rounds and range buckets both count — MaiCaddy does the counting.

03

Nudges you sparingly

A handful of reminders a year, only when a regrip or a fresh glove is genuinely due. Never a daily nag.

04

Values it for insurance

A running total of what your gear is worth, plus a plain-text record you can hand to your insurer in a tap.

05

Finds the right care

Replacement, cleaning, repairs, fitting and regripping — the care each club typically needs, surfaced when it needs it.

03 — SET-UP

Three questions.
Then nothing.

Answer them once and MaiCaddy works from day one — with zero ongoing input. Adjust anytime in Settings, or leave it be.

1

How often do you play?

A few times a year, or most days — a rough answer is plenty.

2

How much do you practise?

Range balls wear grips, gloves and wedges too — so they count.

3

What's your game?

All-round, short-game grinder or driver-heavy — it biases what wears first.
