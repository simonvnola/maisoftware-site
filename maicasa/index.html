Address in. Plan out.

Then, day to day
Everything your home needs. Nothing it doesn't.

A few tabs, one coherent picture of your property. Each one earns its place.

Budget planner

FY-aware recurring + contingency budget. AI estimates costs for upcoming jobs and warns you, gently, if you're heading over.

Photo recognition

Snap a damp patch, a cracked tile, a leaky tap. AI identifies the issue, suggests the right trade, and creates a task with cost guidance.

Contents inventory

Every appliance, fixture and fitting, photographed and tagged. Track current value, warranty, and whether it's depreciable for tax.

DIY guidance

Step-by-step walkthroughs for jobs you can tackle yourself — tools, safety call-outs, time estimates, and the warning signs that mean it's time to call a pro.

Trade briefs & quote tools

When DIY isn't right, get the exact brief to hand a tradesperson — plus side-by-side quote comparison that flags out-of-range pricing.

AI chat assistant

Ask anything about your home — paste a maintenance list to import it, describe a problem, or get advice at 10pm when something looks wrong.
