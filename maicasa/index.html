What Maicasa does
Everything your home needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

A few tabs. One coherent picture of your property. Each one earns its place — and works the moment you open the app.

Prioritised task list
Every job ranked by safety, urgency, season and cost. Open the app, glance at Now, and know exactly what to act on first.
Property scan
Address in, tasks out. Maicasa researches your property — facts, council, climate risks, ongoing costs — then generates a tailored maintenance plan.
Inspection report import
Drop in a building inspection PDF and every finding becomes a tracked, prioritised task — instantly. No more re-typing 60-page reports.
Budget planner
FY-aware recurring + contingency budget. AI estimates costs for upcoming jobs and warns you, gently, if you're heading over.
Photo recognition
Snap a damp patch, a cracked tile, a leaky tap. AI identifies the issue, suggests the right trade, and creates a task with cost guidance.
Contents inventory
Every appliance, fixture and fitting, photographed and tagged. Track current value, warranty, and whether it's depreciable for tax.
DIY guidance
Step-by-step walkthroughs for jobs you can tackle yourself — tools needed, safety call-outs, time estimates and the warning signs that mean it's time to call a pro.
Trade briefs & quote tools
When DIY isn't right, get the exact brief to hand a tradesperson — what to ask for, what to watch out for, plus side-by-side quote comparison that flags out-of-range pricing.
AI chat assistant
Ask anything about your home — paste a maintenance list to import it, describe a problem, or get advice at 10pm when something looks wrong.

Works with your other Mai apps

The things you already track in MaiCellar and MaiCaddy can appear in your contents inventory as linked collections — a single summary row each, counted into your insurance total. You give consent in those apps; Maicasa just shows the value.

Your wine cellar
Switch on sharing in MaiCellar and your cellar's total value appears as one linked row, refreshed as your collection changes. Individual wines stay in MaiCellar.
Your golf bag
Tap to share in MaiCaddy and your bag's value drops in as a dated snapshot. The gear list and photos stay on your phone.
